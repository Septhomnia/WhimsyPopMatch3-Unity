## Internship Development Diary — Whimsy Pop Match-3 Game

# Day 23 - Menu Animations

*Date: 22 July 2026

Today I was working on the menu animations for my match 3 game. 

I used the Mecanim technology from Unity to animate the UI panels I animated the fading panel and the panel that shows game info.When the player clicks on the OK button, both panels slide off the screen. I also wrote a FadePanelController script for fading animation to be after clicking OK button. It controls the Animator components and sets the “Out” bool to true when the OK button is hit.

Once the button was connected in Unity, the panels played the animation correctly.Initially I had a problem with the panel position values. I had different numbers than the tutorial, but that was because my Canvas layout was different. Then I thought I should enter the panel position of own, without copying the instructional data directly.

Animation works when I fixed the keyframes and the RectTransform placements. The menu feels more fluid and the game feels more rounded.
